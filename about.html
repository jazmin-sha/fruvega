FAQ Questions

What products does Fruvega offer?

Fruvega specializes in premium dehydrated vegetables, vegetable powders, and vacuum-fried chips. Our products are perfect for healthy snacking, cooking, and meal prep — without compromising on nutrition or flavor.

Where can I buy Fruvega products?

Fruvega products are available exclusively on Amazon in India, the United States, and Canada. Just search for “Fruvega” on your local Amazon store to explore our full range.

Are Fruvega products healthy?

Absolutely! Our dehydrated and vacuum-fried products retain maximum nutrients while using minimal or no oil. We don’t add preservatives or artificial flavors — just real vegetables with real taste.

How long do Fruvega products last?

Thanks to our dehydration and vacuum-sealing technology, Fruvega products have a long shelf life — typically 6 to 12 months. Just store them in a cool, dry place and reseal properly after opening.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, Fruvega operates and delivers through Amazon in India, the US, and Canada. We’re working on expanding to more regions soon — stay tuned!
